1. Where we ship

We ship to most countries worldwide. If we cannot deliver to your address we will cancel the order and refund you in full.

2. Shipping options and times

  • Standard delivery: US$9.99 (free on product orders over US$99).
  • Express delivery: US$14.99.
  • Next-day delivery: US$29.99, where available.

Orders are usually prepared within 3–7 business days (plus 2–3 days for name and number printing). Delivery times after dispatch depend on the destination, the courier and customs, and are estimates, not guarantees. We will send tracking details when available.

3. Customs and import charges

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes and handling fees in your country. These are not included in our prices and are paid by you to the courier or customs authority.

4. Cancelling an order (right of withdrawal)

If you are a consumer, you may cancel a distance purchase within 14 days from the day you receive the goods (or from the day you receive the order confirmation, whichever is later), by emailing hagaydardik@gmail.com with your order number. Return the goods unused, unwashed, with tags and in their original packaging.

  • For consumers in Israel, under the Consumer Protection Law, a cancellation fee of 5% of the price or ILS 100, whichever is lower, may be charged, except where the law says otherwise (for example cancellation because of a defect or non-delivery). Certain consumers (such as people with disabilities, senior citizens and new immigrants, as defined by law) may have an extended cancellation period of up to 4 months.
  • For consumers in other countries, the mandatory withdrawal rules of your country apply where they give you more protection.
  • Exceptions: as permitted by law, products personalised to your request (name, number or patches) cannot be cancelled once production has started, unless defective. Opened mystery boxes can only be returned if defective.

Unless the goods are defective or not as described, the cost of returning them is paid by you. We refund the price paid (and the original standard shipping cost where required by law), less any lawful cancellation fee, within 14 days of receiving the returned goods or proof of return, to the original payment method.

5. Defective, damaged or wrong items

If your item arrives defective, damaged or different from what you ordered, email hagaydardik@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We will offer a replacement or a full refund, including shipping costs, after reviewing the claim.

6. Lost parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 30 days, or the courier confirms the parcel is lost, contact us and we will resend the item or refund you. We are not responsible for losses caused by an incorrect address entered by the customer, or for parcels refused or not collected.

7. Game codes and digital items

Mundo XI codes included with orders are free extras. They have no cash value and are not refundable. If you cancel an order, any codes from that order may be cancelled and the related Virtual Items removed.
