Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · Shipping & Returns · Giveaway Rules · Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to shop and play. We are working to make futbolmundoshop.com accessible in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, as reflected in Israeli Standard IS 5568.

What we have done

  • Keyboard navigation for the main store pages and checkout.
  • Text alternatives for important images and buttons.
  • Readable contrast and scalable text; the site works with browser zoom.
  • The site is available in several languages, including Hebrew and Arabic with right-to-left layout.
  • The Mundo XI 3D views have a 2D option, and animations respect the “reduce motion” setting of your device.

Known limitations

Some parts of the Mundo XI game (especially the real-time match controls and 3D views) and some supplier product photos may not yet be fully accessible. Payment pages are provided by PayPal and follow PayPal's accessibility standards.

Contact the accessibility coordinator

If you have difficulty using any part of the site, or want to suggest an improvement, email hagaydardik@gmail.com. Please describe the problem and the page. We will reply as soon as possible and offer help completing your order by another way if needed.
