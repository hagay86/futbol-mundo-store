This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with and what rights you have. It applies to futbolmundoshop.com, the online store and the Mundo XI game. The data controller is Hagay Dardik, an individual business registered in Israel, trading as “Futbol Mundo” (“Futbol Mundo”, “we”). Contact: hagaydardik@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: email address, optional manager name and password (stored only as a secure one-way hash).
  • Orders: items, sizes, chosen customisation (names, numbers, patches), delivery name, address, country and phone number, order history and amounts.
  • Support and giveaways: messages you send us and information needed to verify and deliver prizes.

Data collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, approximate country (used to choose the site language and default delivery country), browser and device type, pages and error logs, collected by our hosting provider for security and operation.
  • Game data: your Mundo XI progress (cards, squad, results, Mundo Credits, codes redeemed), stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in our database.
  • Cookies and local storage: a secure session cookie that keeps you signed in, and local storage on your device for your bag, wishlist, language, delivery details and game save. We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data. See section 8.

Data from third parties

  • PayPal sends us payment confirmations, the payer's email and, where applicable, the shipping address. We never receive your full card number.

2. Why we use your data and our legal bases

  • To provide your account, the store and the Game, and to process and deliver orders (performance of a contract).
  • To send service emails such as order confirmations, game codes, password resets and order updates (contract).
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse, and to keep the Services secure (legitimate interests; legal obligation).
  • To keep accounting and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To run giveaways and deliver prizes (contract / your participation).
  • To improve the Services using aggregated, non-identifying information (legitimate interests).
  • To send marketing only where you agreed or where the law otherwise allows (consent / legitimate interests). You can opt out at any time.

You are not legally obliged to give us personal data, but without the data marked as required we cannot create an account or deliver an order.

3. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as needed with service providers acting on our behalf, under contracts that protect your data:

  • PayPal (payment processing, fraud prevention). PayPal acts as an independent controller for payment data under its own privacy statement.
  • Vercel Inc. (website hosting and server logs, USA/EU).
  • Upstash (our database for accounts, orders and game saves).
  • Resend (sending service emails).
  • Our suppliers and couriers (name, delivery address and phone number, to make and deliver your order).
  • Content delivery networks used to load fonts, images and scripts (for example Google Fonts, cdnjs and our suppliers' image servers), which may receive your IP address when your browser loads them.
  • Authorities and advisers where required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is sold or reorganised.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. International transfers

We operate from Israel and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: while your account is active, and up to 24 months after your last activity, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Order and invoice records: 7 years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Server logs: typically up to 30 days.
  • Giveaway entries: until the draw for that month is completed and any prize is delivered, plus a reasonable period for disputes.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, the EU/UK GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to certain processing; receive a copy in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time; and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. To exercise them, email hagaydardik@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will respond within the time required by law (usually 30 days). You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Israel this is the Privacy Protection Authority.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, secure HttpOnly session cookies, access controls and reputable providers. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.
